## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The formatter never renames
The formatter SHALL change layout only and SHALL NOT change the spelling of any identifier, in source files and in programs generated by Mods. The token check SHALL compare identifiers exactly and SHALL fail a format run that changes one. Renaming to a naming profile SHALL be the work of the naming-style lint and its quick fix.

#### Scenario: Snake_case item is kept
- **GIVEN** `use` and item names written in snake_case
- **WHEN** `beskid fmt` runs
- **THEN** the identifiers keep their spelling

#### Scenario: Test name is kept
- **GIVEN** `test parse_empty { }`
- **WHEN** `beskid fmt` runs
- **THEN** the name stays `parse_empty`, and the naming lint reports W1637
