The [Platform specification](/platform-spec/) is large on purpose; languages are not one PDF. This chapter is a **map reader's guide**: where home tiles live, how domains split, and when to open an architecture graph instead of guessing crate names.

<Aside type="tip">

Start from a **feature hub** `index.mdx`, not a random article. Hubs carry scope, status, and decision indexes.

</Aside>

<Aside type="note">

The Book explains how to navigate and apply the standard. A linked platform-spec page is the authority for requirements, scenarios, and maturity; this chapter deliberately does not restate them.

</Aside>

## A repeatable route through the standard

Start with a domain, narrow to an area and feature hub, then read the relevant requirement. Only after that should you follow architecture graphs or implementation anchors. This makes it easier to distinguish a language guarantee from a compiler, runtime, or tooling concern.
