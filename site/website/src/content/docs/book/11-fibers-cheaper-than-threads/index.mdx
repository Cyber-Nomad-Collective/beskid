Threads are expensive. **async/await** is threads with extra syntax and conference talks. Beskid uses **`spawn`** and **`Fiber<T>`**—cooperative fibers, channel-only cross-fiber data, and no `async` keyword because we have seen your previous codebase.

Normative: [Fibers and spawn](/platform-spec/language-meta/evaluation/fibers-and-spawn/). Runtime: [Fiber scheduler and stacks](/platform-spec/execution/runtime/fiber-scheduler-and-stacks/). API: [Concurrency package](/platform-spec/core-library/concurrency/concurrency-package/).

<Aside type="caution">

Reserved `async` / `await` tokens are **compile errors**. Do not `#define` around the language; use `spawn`.

</Aside>
