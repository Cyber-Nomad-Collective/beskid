# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Add Book to the platform family and show nine enlarged integrated service icons in the preview.

- Interactive Ridge preview with Forest, Pine, Moss and Emerald green pairs and whole-page light/dark modes.

- Regularize Tracker’s stair spacing, replace overlapping Nexus regions with one masked body, and refine Core, Book, and Learn terminals to remove small protrusions.

- Apply Emerald primary/reversed/supporting colors while preserving brand ink, paper and the existing UI-surface recommendation.
- Compose all nine service marks across both peaks using polygon bodies and luminance masks with transparent structural channels; preserve the parent Ridge and document service meanings and inline mask IDs.

- Focus the preview on the user-selected Ridge identity and remove the other concept artwork. Emerald is the approved production palette; alternative greens remain exploratory previews.

- Generate the PDF specimen and Lottie reveal from the shared solid logo polygons, removing the obsolete mesh design and duplicated PDF guideline prose.
- Use pnpm throughout the complete asset generation command.

### Fixed

- Restrict cleanup to known generated assets, preserving package metadata, TypeScript configuration, and unrelated files.
- Keep Lottie artwork in the same coordinates as SVG and finish the reveal at the complete static mark.
